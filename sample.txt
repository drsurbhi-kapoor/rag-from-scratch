One of the most humble and visionary industrial giants of India is the Ratan Tata. 
As the chairman of Tata group he converted a conventional commercial conglomerate into an international titan with operations in every sector ranging from steel to automobile. 
However, contrary to many rich dilettantes who merely leaned on their family’s affection to build their success, Tata was not born with a silver spoon in his mouth.
He began his career at the lowest level on the operational value chain of Tata Steel, aggregating functional knowledge of the company from the grassroots level. 
This acquired knowledge helped him to gain experience in industry as well as in the workforce.
With the leadership of Ratan Tata at the helm, Tata Group also took on a marvellous industrial makeover. 
He was instrumental in many path breaking deals like Jaguar Land Rover and Corus Steel which put Indian industry on the world map.
What makes him stand out, however, is that much of his work is aimed at social good and social solutions.
He introduced the Tata nano, a car that is an affordable way into car owning for the man on the street. 
Everyone understood his good intention of serving the larger community of the project despite the many challenges affecting the project.
Ratan Tata has been not only a great businessman but also an ethical leader, and philanthropist as well. 
He continues to inspire young entrepreneurs and students with his mantra: “I don’t believe in taking the right decisions. 
I do decide things and then I make that decision correct .” 
He proves that one can come from anywhere, it is not necessarily your privilege that gives birth to success but your value, humility and how you use your business to impact the world.
